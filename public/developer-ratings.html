Why this must exist

A ₹1 crore decision deserves more than a brochure and a sales pitch.

Before you buy, every developer has a story about the home you will receive.

After owners move in, the market rarely records whether that story came true.

The evidence exists. It is simply scattered across WhatsApp groups, association meetings and thousands of private memories.

Lost in memory“We were promised June. We got the keys in February.”

Possession experience should become a pattern buyers can inspect.

Lost in groups“The sample flat and the delivered fittings were not the same.”

Promise-versus-delivery should be structured, not buried in chat history.

Lost after handover“The sale was smooth. The service afterwards was not.”

After-sales experience belongs in the buying decision.

What FlatData is building

Not another star rating. A record a buyer can question.

A single score hides too much. FlatData will separate the experience into the questions that matter—and show the amount, freshness and verification of the evidence behind every answer.

01Possession timeline

Expected date, actual handover and how communication changed.

02Build quality

Finishing, common areas, defects and how the home aged.

03Price parity

Whether the final cost remained close to the signed schedule.

04Promises kept

What the brochure showed, what the agreement said and what arrived.

05After-sales support

Responsiveness, snag resolution and service after the keys.

06Evidence strength

Review count, project spread, freshness and verified-owner share.

How the record earns trust

Owners contribute experience. FlatData contributes discipline.

The goal is not to create a complaint wall. It is to turn lived experience into structured, careful intelligence that helps the next buyer ask better questions.

01Structured questions

Feedback stays focused on delivery, quality, price, promises and service.

02Private verification

Ownership evidence is checked without publishing a person’s unit or identity.

03Visible evidence

Every pattern shows how many people, projects and recent experiences support it.

04Right to respond
