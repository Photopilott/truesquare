Published rules

Feedback loops need guardrails.

The module is designed to make buyers and owners smarter without turning anonymous opinion into unquestioned truth.

01

Evidence before averages

Every score carries its review count, project spread, freshness, and verified-owner share. Thin evidence stays visibly thin.

02

Structured, not sensational

Reviews focus on possession, quality, parity, fittings, promises, and service. Personal attacks and unverifiable claims do not belong.

03

Right to respond

Developers may add a dated public response, but cannot pay to remove criticism, improve a score, or rank above another builder.
